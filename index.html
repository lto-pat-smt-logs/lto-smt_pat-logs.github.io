🧭 CPAO Ticket Resolution & Endorsement Workflow

STEP 1

Intake & Logging

Log incoming tickets from Facebook, IMKC, CDMPAO, Mailbox, 8888, ARTA, PACe, or CCB with accurate receipt dates and concern categories.

STEP 2

Triage & 24H Timer

Classify Simple, Complex, or Highly Technical tickets. Enable the 24-hour reminder flag on active pending items needing follow-up.

STEP 3

Regional Endorsement

For endorsed concerns, generate a formal letter with 20-working-day EODB deadline calculation and attach supporting PDFs/images.

STEP 4

Closure & Reporting

Mark resolved tickets as Closed with complete response details, then compile accomplishment and performance reports.
